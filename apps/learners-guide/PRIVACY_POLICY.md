# Privacy Policy

## Overview
Learner's Guide handles educational information to provide student management services.

## Data Collection
The platform may store account information, student details, attendance, fees, results, and educational content required for operation.

## Authentication
Accounts are managed through secure authentication services. Users should protect their login credentials.

## Data Storage
Data is stored using secured cloud infrastructure with access controls and row-level security policies.

## Third-party Services
The platform may use services such as Supabase and hosting providers to operate the application.

## User Rights
Users can request information about their stored data and correction of inaccurate information through the administrator.
